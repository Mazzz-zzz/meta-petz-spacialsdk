## Long Description (Markdown)

## A Pet That Lives In Your Room

MetaPetz transforms your living space into a playground for your virtual companion. Using Meta Quest's room understanding, your pet sees the same space you do—walking on your actual floor, navigating around your couch, and even jumping up onto your furniture.

## Intelligent Navigation

Your pet doesn't just walk in straight lines. With A* pathfinding and full room awareness, it navigates intelligently around obstacles, finds the best route to any destination, and even jumps onto elevated surfaces like tables and couches.

**Point-to-Move**: Point anywhere in your room and your pet will find its way there, avoiding furniture and walls along the path.

## Play Fetch

Grab a bone toy with your hand and give it a toss. Watch it bounce off your real walls with physics simulation, then see your pet sprint after it. When your pet catches up, it picks up the bone in its mouth and carries it around—just like a real dog.

- **Physics-based throwing** with realistic bouncing
- **Hand tracking** for natural grab-and-throw
- **Bone carrying** behavior with mouth attachment

## Tamagotchi-Style Care

Your pet needs attention to thrive:

- **Hunger** - Feed your pet to keep it happy
- **Happiness** - Play to boost mood
- **Health** - Heal when your companion needs care

Watch stats change in real-time. Neglect your pet and it will let you know. Take good care and watch it flourish.

## Progression & Customization

- **Earn XP** through every interaction
- **Level up** your pet over time
- **Customize colors** - change coat, eyes, and accessories
- **Choose your pet** from a selection of companions

## Cloud Save

Your pet's stats, level, and customization sync to the cloud. Pick up right where you left off, every time.

## Key Features

- Full room understanding with MRUK integration
- Physics-based bone throwing with wall collisions
- A* pathfinding around furniture
- Jump animations for elevated surfaces
- Hand tracking interactions
- Real-time stat decay and care system
- XP progression and leveling
- Color customization
- Firebase cloud persistence
