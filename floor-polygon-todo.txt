FLOOR POLYGON LIMITATION - TODO
===============================

Current Implementation:
- Floor polygon is extracted from MRUK FLOOR anchor's min/max bounds
- This creates a RECTANGULAR polygon even for irregular rooms

Problem:
- L-shaped, T-shaped, or other irregular floor layouts won't be accurately represented
- The polygon will be the bounding rectangle of the floor, not the actual shape

Why it still works:
- Wall physics colliders enforce the actual room shape
- Pet can't walk through walls even if floor polygon allows that area

Future Fix:
- Find the actual boundary vertices API in Meta Spatial SDK
- Possible properties to investigate:
  - MRUKPlane.planeBoundary2D (exists in Unity MRUK)
  - MRUKAnchor boundary data
  - Room mesh vertices from MRUK
- May need to build polygon from wall corner positions instead
